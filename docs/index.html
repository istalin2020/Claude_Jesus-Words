About the App

Jesus Words brings you 365 authentic spoken words of Jesus Christ from the New Testament — one verse for each day of the year, in both English and Tamil.

  • 365 daily Bible verses spoken by Jesus
  • English & Tamil language support
  • Optional daily morning reminders
  • Beautiful shareable verse images
  • Verse history to revisit past blessings
  • Multiple beautiful themes

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the language?

Tap the language toggle (En / த) at the top right of the home screen to switch between English and Tamil.

How do I set or change my daily reminder?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and use the time picker to choose your preferred reminder time. Tap "Update Reminder" to save.

Are notifications required?

No. Push notifications are completely optional. You can use the app without enabling notifications. You can enable or disable them at any time in Settings.

How do I share a verse?

Tap the "Share This Blessing" button below the daily verse to generate a beautiful image you can share via Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram, and more.

Which Bible translation is used?

English verses are from the New International Version (NIV). Tamil verses are from the Thiruviviliam (Common Tamil Bible).

Does the app collect my data?

No. Jesus Words does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All preferences are stored locally on your device.
